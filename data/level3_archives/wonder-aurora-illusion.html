Some archivists suggest that Ørsted was coerced by Napoleonic authorities to withhold his findings, fearing that revealing this knowledge might destabilize existing power structures reliant on "natural mysteries."

Fast forward to the early 20th century, where the enigmatic Nikola Tesla reportedly constructed an auroral generator, capable of mimicking natural auroras on a smaller scale. Tesla's plans, however, were lost in the labyrinth of posthumous government confiscations—a familiar tale for those who tread too close to the borders of permissible science.
