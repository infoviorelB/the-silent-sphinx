Lucius Glow heard the music of the spheres in silence.

Glow's fascination with mathematics extended to the practical, yet no less sacred, practice of geometry. The golden ratio, revered by artists and architects alike, captivated him. Though few of Glow's blueprints survived, declassified documents hint that his work influenced secretive architectural projects in early 20th-century government buildings.

His philosophical inquiries were deeply influenced by the Hermetic tradition, a syncretic belief system that blends elements of Greek, Egyptian, and Judaic thought.
