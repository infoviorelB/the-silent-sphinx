Theirs was a vision fueled by a unique blend of philosophy, mathematics, and esotericism.

According to newly uncovered texts, the Hashashin went beyond the teachings of Pythagoras and ventured into uncharted territories, exploring how numbers could serve as keys to unlocking the metaphysical structures of reality. Such cryptic numerological endeavors were not merely academic exercises. The Hashashin believed that certain numbers held the power to influence the physical world. Their secret meetings, often carried out at midnight under the cloak of darkness, explored the divine properties of shapes like the hexagon and the spiral, which they believed could unlock the secrets of infinite wisdom.
